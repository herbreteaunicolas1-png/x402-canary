# X402 Factory — Sales Method V2

Status: ACTIVE

The factory optimizes for independently evidenced buyer demand, repeat use, contribution margin and agent-native distribution — not endpoint count or raw x402 traffic.

A candidate is `VALUE + BUYER + CHANNEL + PAYMENT MODE + MARGIN + REPEAT LOOP`.

Hard NO-BUILD/HOLD if demand proof is mainly raw calls or one seller; independent buyer breadth is absent; the function is cheaply reproducible locally with no external value; no buyer-native channel/repeat loop exists; gross margin or upstream rights are unclear; discovery cannot express a callable example; failure can charge for unusable output; or channel attribution is absent.

Evidence hierarchy: independent revenue/buyers > repeat distinct buyers > multiple sellers with paid demand > router/marketplace selection > public buyer integrations > raw marketplace calls/self-reported metrics.

Distribution requires at least one buyer-native install/consumption path plus free discovery metadata. Charge for execution/value, not for discovering what the tool does.

Every paid route must continuously measure valid 402 behavior, paid-call success, latency, uptime, schema/example validity, price, trust and last independent payment.

The 0.5.0 experiment is limited to Dependency Gate and Release Gate. Scale only after independent payer and repeat-use evidence.
